Sunlit Earth for macOS
======================

Sunlit Earth is a free and open source desktop application that renders a
beautiful view of Earth as seen from space and sets it as your wallpaper.
The rendered image updates in the background, reflecting the current time of
day, cloud cover, the positions of the Sun, Moon, and stars, and other
real-world conditions.

There are two downloads for each Mac, "aarch64" for Apple Silicon and
"x86_64" for Intel. Both hold the same app:

  *-app.zip          Sunlit Earth.app, for Finder and the Applications folder
  *-terminal.tar.gz  the plain program, for starting it from Terminal

The app
-------

1. Unzip the download and move "Sunlit Earth.app" to Applications.
2. Double-click it. macOS says it could not verify that the app is free of
   malware, because the app is not notarized by Apple.
3. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Sunlit Earth. macOS may ask for your password.

Instead of step 3 you can run this once in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Sunlit Earth.app"

From Terminal
-------------

Downloaded with curl and unpacked with tar, the program starts without the
dialog above and prints its log to the terminal:

    curl -L -O https://github.com/sunlit-earth/sunlit-earth/releases/latest/download/<file name>
    tar xzf sunlit-earth-*-terminal.tar.gz
    cd sunlit-earth-*-terminal/
    ./sunlit-earth

A tarball downloaded in a browser and unpacked in Finder gets the same
dialog as the app. Keep the files together: sunlit-earth finds its Earth
images in the textures folder next to it.

Using it
--------

Choose a view, click "Set as Wallpaper", and enable "Auto-refresh wallpaper"
to keep the wallpaper up to date. Settings, caches and log files are stored
in ~/Library/Application Support/SunlitEarth.
